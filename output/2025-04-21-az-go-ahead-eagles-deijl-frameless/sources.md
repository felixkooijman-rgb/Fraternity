## Confirmed

- AZ 1-1 Go Ahead Eagles after extra time, Go Ahead Eagles won 4-2 on penalties – first KNVB Beker in club history (NOS, nl.wikipedia, fcupdate, Salland1)
- Monday 21.04.2025, De Kuip, Rotterdam, KNVB Beker final; attendance 42,972 (nl.wikipedia, ga-eagles.nl)
- Parrott scored a retaken penalty at 54' (VI, The42, Voetbalprimeur)
- Deijl equalised from the penalty spot in the 99th minute = 90+9' after Koopmeiners handball (nl.wikipedia, Salland1, NOS)
- Deijl wore #2 for Go Ahead Eagles in 2024-25 (ESPN, Sofascore, round-31 Eredivisie line-up)
- Starting XIs (Voetbalzone, fcupdate, nl.wikipedia)
- Go Ahead Eagles played the final in a special purple away shirt (ga-eagles.nl, Salland1)
