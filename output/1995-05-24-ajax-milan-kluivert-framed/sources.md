## Confirmed

- Ajax 1-0 AC Milan, 24.05.1995, Ernst-Happel-Stadion Vienna, Champions League final (UEFA, Wikipedia)
- Kluivert came on as a substitute (~69'-70') and scored at 85' (UEFA, BeSoccer, fandom)
- Kluivert #15 in the final (BeSoccer line-up, IMDb match listing)
- Ajax wore the navy Umbro away kit as nominal home side; Milan in white (sartorial.soccer, Wikipedia)
